# Requirements: Validate and load the Marsden records

## Covers
- **PRD requirements:** FR-07 (experimental evidence: reproduce the Marsden comparisons), FR-12 (traceability), NFR-01 (reproducibility), NFR-03 (auditability).
- **Roadmap exit check satisfied (DE part):** "DE validates the supplementary records with Pandera ... before they are used." DS2's part is the test on 74.5 / 56.6 / 40.8 kg N/ha/yr applied N and 1.925 / 0.261 / 0.195 kg/ha/yr herbicide.

## Decisions (2026-11-15)
- Scope: validate DS2's outputs, load the management data and his 4 lineage rows, load the reconciliation record. Out: any change to his reproduction, the fix of his schema tests (14 error without raw files), claims for the validation summary (Phase 14).
- One long table with an `indicator_id` per row.
- The lineage gap (12 fields against 15 columns) is filled by his own runner's convention.
- Validation: offline golden tests plus a live reproduction that skips without the raw files.

## Inputs
- The five raw files at `s3://<bucket>/raw/marsden/pone.0047149-supplement/2026-11-13/` (also `data/raw/marsden/...`), with his manifest `transform/marsden/manifests/2026-11-13.json`.
- DS2's outputs in `data/out/marsden/` (regenerated by `python -m transform.marsden.marsden_repro`): `management_by_year.csv` (27 rows), `management_summary.csv` (3), `reconciliation.csv` (20), `indicator_lineage.json` (4), `run_manifest.json`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. His outputs pass his Pandera schemas and the DE's schemas; a failure stops the load and no value is edited.
2. The loaded nine-year values equal the PRD targets under his rule (within half of the last digit): applied N 74.5, 56.6, 40.8 and herbicide 1.925, 0.261, 0.195; all 12 PRD reconciliation checks load as `MATCH`.
3. The one `UNRECONCILED` check (2-yr manufactured N, 74.5 from Table S3 against 80 in the paper's text) is loaded as `UNRECONCILED` with its note, and the diagnostic (2011 as maize alone, 80.7) as `INFO`. Nothing is corrected.
4. The wide-to-long conversion is lossless: all 108 + 12 values appear once, and in every row `applied_n = manufactured_n + manure_n` still holds.
5. Every `indicator_id` in `marsden_management` has a lineage row, else the load is refused; loading his 4 lineage rows leaves DS2's 21 rows unchanged.
6. The SHA-256 of each of the five input files in his run manifest equals the checksum in its S3 manifest.
7. Loads are idempotent (identical table hashes on a second run); `make check` passes without raw files or S3 (live checks skip).

## Evidence labels, scale and limitations to preserve
- `experimental`: one research site (Marsden Farm, Boone County, Iowa), nine seasons, treatment-level table values, not plot-level records.
- Applied N is manufactured plus manure N and excludes biological N fixation; manure N is total N, not plant-available; herbicide is mass of active ingredient, not toxicity.
- The results are not a field-level prediction and no soil-carbon, erosion or nitrate claim is made. Yield, profit and weed claims stay attributed to the paper.

## Out of scope
Changing the reproduction or its thresholds, new reconciliation checks, the API and the page that shows the record, and the fix of his schema tests.
