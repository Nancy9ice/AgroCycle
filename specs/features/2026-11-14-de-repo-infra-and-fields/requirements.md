# Requirements: Repo, infra and field GeoJSON setup

## Covers
- **PRD requirements:** FR-01, NFR-01, NFR-06
- **Roadmap exit check satisfied:** everyone can run the repo; the primary demo field and the test-set polygons are committed as GeoJSON with their provenance recorded.

## Scope and decisions
- In scope: repo layout, env handling, S3 and ClickHouse connectivity, field GeoJSON schema, validation and commit.
- The bucket and ClickHouse credentials already exist; nothing is provisioned.
- The BE/FE API contract is a separate task, not this spec.

## Inputs
- Env variables: `AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `AWS_REGION`, `S3_BUCKET`, `CLICKHOUSE_HOST`, `CLICKHOUSE_PORT`, `CLICKHOUSE_USER`, `CLICKHOUSE_PASSWORD`, `CLICKHOUSE_SECURE`, `CLICKHOUSE_DATABASE` (always `agrocycle`: all project tables live there)
- Polygons generated by DE from CDL 2015-2025 clips (WGS84, EPSG:4326 in the output); SSURGO Soil Data Access for the soil at each field centre
- Story and Boone County boundaries (public Census TIGER)

## Outputs
`data/fields/fields.geojson`: one Feature per field, with properties:

| Property | Type | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `field_id` | string | unique, e.g. `primary-001`, `test-014` |
| `role` | enum | `primary` or `test`; exactly one `primary` |
| `boundary_source` | enum | `demo` or `real` |
| `county` | enum | `Story` or `Boone` |
| `provenance` | string | who drew it, how and when |
| `domain_note` | string | in-domain, or out-of-domain type (continuous corn, pasture, non-rotation) |
| `cover_detail` | string, optional | pasture-hay fields only (required there, null elsewhere): `grass-pasture`, `alfalfa`, `other-hay` or `mixed-grass-hay` (the cover in at least 9 of 11 years, else mixed) |

The yearly crop sequences in `field_selection_notes.csv` use separate categories: C corn, S soybeans, G grass/pasture, A alfalfa, H other hay, O oats, L clover, X other. The field classes above are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A fresh clone plus a local env file runs `make check` successfully.
2. `smoke_s3.py` uploads, downloads and checksums a small test object under a `smoke/` prefix.
3. `smoke_clickhouse.py` connects to the `agrocycle` database, runs `SELECT 1`, and creates, queries and drops a scratch table in it.
4. `fields.geojson` has one `primary` and at least 30 `test` fields, split across both counties.
5. Every polygon is valid, non-empty and inside its stated county. Invalid ones fail with a clear message.
6. Every field has all properties above. All `demo` boundaries are labelled `demo`.
7. Several out-of-domain fields exist, noted in `domain_note`.
8. No secret, raw data or env file is committed.

## Evidence labels, scale and limitations to preserve
- Fields are `assumption/user input`: demo geometry, not real farm boundaries.
- The primary field is a maize-soybean block chosen from CDL (classified output, agreement with another classification, not independent validation). DS1 still checks it.
- Soil series is SSURGO survey information at the field centre point, not an area-weighted field soil.

## Data limitations and suggestions for other roles

These are findings from building the field set. They are suggestions for the owners named, not decisions.

### No fields already growing alfalfa, other hay, oats or clover
- **Finding:** with the 90% uniform-crop rule (every year, 2015-2025), Story and Boone have no blocks dominated by alfalfa, other hay, oats or clover, even among small (about 6 ha) blocks. Every pasture-hay field is grass-pasture. Corn and soybeans dominate, and the crops used by strategies B and C are rare as large uniform parcels. Result from the 30 m clips; to be rechecked once the 10 m files for 2024 and 2025 are in.
- **Why it is not a defect:** the decision starts from an existing 2-year maize-soybean field (strategy A). A field that already grows B or C crops is outside that starting point.
- **Suggestions:**
  - DS1 (Phase 3 rules): define what "history already includes alfalfa, oats or clover" means for the rotation flag.
  - DS2 (Phase 9 rules): make it an applicability rule, so the engine returns "not assessed" or abstains for such a field instead of a comparison.
  - Engine tests: cover this with constructed profiles, not real polygons. The 45 real fields are for end-to-end runs.
  - Missing history is unknown, never neutral or favourable.
- **If real examples are wanted:** loosen the uniformity rule or search smaller blocks. This gives messier fields, so DS1 decides.

### 2024 and 2025 CDL are the resampled 30 m product
- **Finding:** from 2024 the native CDL is 10 m, but every web export we could use (CropScape; CroplandCROS, whose download link returns HTTP 400) gives the resampled 30 m product for 2024 and 2025. USDA publishes the native 10 m only as national files (2024: 9.0 GB, 2025: 9.8 GB). A re-export checked on 2026-11-14 was byte-identical to the earlier 30 m files.
- **Decision (2026-11-14):** use the resampled 30 m for 2024 and 2025 for choosing fields. DS1 first asked for native 10 m, then agreed to stay on 30 m because 10 m proved hard to get.
- **Effect on these fields:** small. Blocks have a 60 m clean margin and are judged by shares, not single pixels. Small crops and field edges are blended more at 30 m, so small alfalfa, oats or clover parcels are less likely to be seen.
- **For Phase 3 (DS1, DE):** record the product and resolution for every year in the crop-history table (30 m native up to 2023, resampled 30 m for 2024-2025). If native 10 m is wanted, it needs the national files, a team decision because it breaks the subset-before-download rule. `scripts/select_fields.py` already works on mixed 30 m and 10 m files (tested on synthetic rasters only, not real 10 m data).

### Financial returns are not modelled
- **Finding:** the main reason farmers keep the 2-year rotation is financial return, but the project does not calculate it. Market and income appear only as farmer constraints and priorities (PRD FR-10 and section 9) and as AMS and NASS market context. PRD section 9 and the mission rule out profit calculations unless yield, input-cost, machinery, labour, transport and price data are available with explicit assumptions. Those data are not in scope.
- **Suggestions:**
  - FE/BE (Phase 11): show "financial returns: not assessed" as its own dimension in the comparison, so the gap is visible.
  - DS2: read what Davis et al. (2012) report on profitability and, if supported, show it as an attributed published finding for that Iowa setting, never as a prediction for the user's field. Yield and profit claims are attributed, not recomputed.
  - Stretch: accept farmer-supplied prices and costs, labelled assumption/user input, with every assumption shown.
  - F: ask how much income drives their rotation decisions (usability and general sense, not Iowa validation).
- **Not changed by this task:** no code or schema here depends on it.

## Out of scope
Raw data extraction, manifest tooling, ClickHouse table creation, the API contract, and the Phase 3 crop-history product. CDL clips are used here only to choose field locations, and the in-domain rule is a working rule that DS1's Phase 3 rule will replace.
